Heyy thangoo!!

Happy birthday, Sam uh. 🤍

Indha website la irukkura ovvoru word-um, ovvoru memory-um, unakku enna sollanum nu romba naala manasula vechurundha feelings dhaan.

Namma epdi close aanom nu yosicha, oru random night Instagram conversation la start aachu. Aana konjam konjama nee enakku romba comfortable-aana person aayita. Un kitta en secrets solla mudinjadhu; un problems-um en kitta share panna. Sila neram nee pakkathula irundha podhum, lonely-a irundha feeling konjam koranjidum. Un kooda irundha sila moments enakku home maari irundhuchu.

Onam la edutha photos, class la serndhu irundha days, calls, namma thaniya meet panna moments, Calicut days — idhellam enakku precious. Naan unakku help pannadhu ellam count panni solla illa. Unna care panna thonuchu, nee happy-a irukkanum nu thonuchu. Adhan panninen. Adhu enakku natural-a irundhuchu.

Apram namakkulla gap vandhuchu. Late replies, misunderstandings, sanda, silence — idhellam enna romba hurt pannuchu. Sometimes naan en feelings-a correct-a handle pannala; kovathula Instagram la pannadhu thappu. Adhukkaaga genuinely sorry, Sam. Unna hurt panna enakku intention illa. Aana intention illa nu sonna mattum hurt disappear aagidathu nu enakku theriyum. I'm sorry for my part in it.

Un life la Gowtham important nu enakku theriyum. Nee happy-a irukkanum, nalla place aaganum, un dreams ellam achieve pannanum nu manasara wish panren. Un relationship-ku naan disturbance-a irukka virumbala. Un happiness-ku naan respect kudukkuren.

Idhu love confession illa, thangoo. Unna oru romba special friend-a miss panren. Namma share pannina emotional bond-a miss panren. Munna maari ellaame irukkanum nu force panna mudiyadhu nu enakku puriyudhu. Aana someday, rendu perukkum comfortable-a irundha, open-a pesi, misunderstandings-a clear panni, konjam konjama nalla friendship rebuild panna mudinja, adhu enakku romba meaningful-a irukkum.

Unakku space venumna adhayum respect panren. Nee udane reply pannanum, same maari feel pannanum, illa edhuvum promise pannanum nu expectation illa. Indha letter un mela burden poda illa; en manasula irundhadha honest-a solla dhaan.

Thank you for the care you showed me, for the laughs, for trusting me with your stories, and for becoming a beautiful part of my college memories. Nee en life la vandhadhu enakku meaningful. Adha naan marakka maaten.

Happy birthday once again, Sam uh, en thangoo. Nalla saapdu, nalla sirichu iru, un dreams-ku full-a effort podu. Unakku edhavadhu kashtam vandha, andha time la unakku en presence comfortable-a irundha, naan friend-a irukka aasai paduren. Andha friendship rendu perukkum nalladha irukkanum — adhu dhaan en wish.

You deserve a life full of peace, love, laughter, and people who make you feel safe. Always choose what makes you genuinely happy. 🤍
